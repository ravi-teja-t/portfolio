## Goal and positioning

The site has one job. An Upwork client clicks your link from a proposal, and within a minute they decide you can solve their problem and invite you to interview, on Upwork.

**Positioning: "Backend engineer who builds production-grade AI automation."** Four services shown as equals read as "does everything", and clients hear "expert at nothing". One story ties them together instead.

Your edge is depth. Many AI freelancers can wire up a chatbot demo. Few have 5+ years of Java, Spring Boot, microservices and AWS behind them. Your agents are built to survive production: auth, retries, monitoring, and real integrations with the client's systems.

| Priority | Service | Role on the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI agents and automation | Lead offer: hero, first service card, flagship case study (AikaIQ) |
| 2 | Backend and APIs | Proof of depth, and a standalone offer |
| 3 | Healthcare integrations (HL7) | Specialist niche with far fewer qualified freelancers |
| 4 | Websites and web apps | Supporting offer, framed as full-stack delivery |

**Identity line under your name:** Backend and AI Automation Engineer · Founder, Ango Digital Tech

**Hero headline options** (pick one, test the others in proposals):

1. I build AI agents that actually run in production. *(recommended)*
2. AI automation, built on backend engineering that holds up.
3. Production-grade AI agents and APIs for growing businesses.

Write every line as what the client gets, not what you know. "Cut support ticket handling time" beats "experienced in LangChain and RAG".

## Case studies

Three strong case studies beat ten thin ones. Upwork's own guide says the same: lead with visuals and focus on measurable results ([Upwork portfolio guide](https://www.upwork.com/resources/portfolio-guide)).

**Which three to feature:**

1. **AikaIQ** (your product): the agentic AI framework and automation API for support and operations. This is your flagship AI proof.
2. **Your best client AI or automation project.**
3. **Your best client backend or healthcare project.** No healthcare client work you can show? Build a small public demo, such as an HL7 message to REST API service, so the healthcare page has proof.

Backend work is invisible, so an architecture diagram is your screenshot. A one to two minute video walkthrough does more than any paragraph.

Ask clients in writing before you name them. Otherwise describe them by type ("a US e-commerce brand"). Never show client code, data or internal dashboards.

## Look and feel

Aim for "senior engineer: calm and precise", not "creative agency". Clean, light, plenty of white space, one accent colour.

| Element | Choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme | Light background, near-black text; dark panels only for code and diagrams | Reads as professional to non-technical buyers |
| Accent | One colour (deep blue or teal), used only for the Hire me on Upwork button | The call to action is never in doubt |
| Fonts | Inter or Geist for text, JetBrains Mono for code | Free, very legible, technical without feeling cold |
| Layout | Content width about 1,100 px, spacing on an 8 px grid | Generous spacing reads as confident |
| Imagery | Your photo, architecture diagrams, product screenshots, short videos | Stock photos of robots and handshakes read as template work |
| Motion | A subtle fade-in on scroll at most | Polish without slowing the page |
| Mobile | Check every page on a phone; hero and button fit the first screen | Some clients will open your link on a phone first |
| Speed | Lighthouse score 95+ on every page | A slow site undercuts a backend engineer |

## Using the site with Upwork without breaking the rules

Build the site with no contact details at all, and point every button to your Upwork profile. Then you can share it in any proposal without putting your account at risk.

**What Upwork's rules say** ([Sharing Information on Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049608113-Sharing-Information-on-Upwork)):

- Before a contract starts, no contact information in your profile, proposals, job posts or invites.
- External portfolio links are allowed in proposals, but all pre-contract communication stays on Upwork.
- Once a contract is in place, you may share contact information.
- Upwork moderators have also said external portfolios with contact info can't go in your Upwork profile ([community answer](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Can-I-include-a-link-to-my-website-on-my-profile-Will-Upwork/m-p/664441)).

Strictly, the help article lets a portfolio you share in a proposal include contact details, as long as you tell the client and keep talking on Upwork. A contact-free site removes that grey area entirely.

**Keep on the site:**

- "Hire me on Upwork" buttons linking to your profile
- A GitHub link, only if your GitHub profile shows no email

**Leave off the site:**

- Email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram
- Contact forms and booking links such as Calendly
- LinkedIn, X or Instagram links, since they allow direct messages
- Any chatbot that asks for an email address

**Using it day to day:**

1. In each proposal, put the matching service page or case study in the portfolio link space, not the homepage.
2. Mirror your three case studies into Upwork's own portfolio section, with images uploaded directly. Upwork also lets you privately publish items and share them only in proposals ([Upwork portfolio guide](https://www.upwork.com/resources/portfolio-guide)).
3. Add a tag to each link, such as `?src=upwork-chatbot`, and watch your analytics to see which proposals get clicks.

## Tech stack and build

Build it yourself in Next.js. The site then doubles as proof for your web app service, and every page can still render as fast static HTML.

| Layer | Pick | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework | Next.js with Tailwind CSS | The site itself shows clients your Next.js work |
| Content | One MDX file per case study and service page | Add a case study without touching code |
| Hosting | Cloudflare Pages | Free tier, global CDN |
| Analytics | Cloudflare Web Analytics | Free and cookieless, so no cookie banner |
| Diagrams | Excalidraw or draw.io, exported as SVG | Clean architecture visuals for case studies |
| Video | Short MP4s or unlisted YouTube | Keep each walkthrough under two minutes |
| Domain | Your name, e.g. raviteja.dev | Check availability; a name domain outlasts any niche |

**Your differentiator: a live AI demo.** Add an "Ask my AI" assistant, built on AikaIQ, that answers questions about your services and case studies. It proves your main skill on the spot. It must never ask for contact details, and every answer ends with the Upwork button.
